Three sample objects, ready to drop into your physical space. On a phone, tap "View in AR" — on desktop, you get full orbit and zoom instead. No app, no install.

OBJ_01 · ASTRONAUT

Astronaut

GLB · ~2.4 MB · no iOS file (Quick Look unavailable)

A full character mesh with PBR materials — good test of detail and scale against real furniture.

OBJ_02 · SNEAKER

Sneaker

GLB · multi-variant materials

Small, product-scale object — the kind of thing a retail or e-commerce AR demo would actually ship.

OBJ_03 · ROBOT

Robot (animated)

GLB · skeletal animation · idle loop

Carries a baked animation clip — useful for showing that AR objects don't have to be static.

How this works — each <model-viewer> tag loads a .glb file and renders it with WebGL. The "View in AR" path checks, in order: WebXR (Chrome/Android), Scene Viewer (Android fallback), then Quick Look (iOS, needs a .usdz — not included for these three models, so iOS will fall back to the 3D view).

To swap in your own model — replace the src attribute with a path to your .glb, and add ios-src="yourmodel.usdz" if you want Quick Look support on iPhone.
